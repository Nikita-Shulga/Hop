          Internet access

          Your devices meet through this server (free on Cloudflare); the video, control and password then go directly between them, encrypted end to end. The server is private: it only answers at this address (with its key) — anyone else gets “Not found”. Your other computers pick it up with “Use it”; your phone gets it from the Phone QR code.

            About

            Local View lets your phone, tablet or another computer see and control this one. On the same Wi-Fi, open the address and enter the PIN. From anywhere, turn on Internet access and use the ID and password.

            On Wi-Fi nothing leaves your network. Over the internet, devices connect directly and encrypted; the connection server only introduces them. Files your devices send land in the shared folder.

            Use the same PIN on all your computers to hop between them from the device list. Never forward this port on your router — use Internet access instead.
