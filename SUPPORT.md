# Getting help

**Something doesn't work?** First check the [FAQ in the README](README.md#faq) and the [troubleshooting section of the guide](docs/GUIDE.md#troubleshooting).

**Still stuck?** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/Nikita-Shulga/Hop/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Say which computer you were sharing, what you connected from, whether it was on Wi-Fi or over the internet, and what happened. Please leave out your computer ID, password and addresses.

**Have an idea?** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/Nikita-Shulga/Hop/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

**Found a security problem?** Don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Hop is maintained in spare time, so replies can take a few days.
